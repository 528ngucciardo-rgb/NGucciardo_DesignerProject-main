Yusaku Kamekura

Early Life

Yusaku Kamekura was born on April 6, 1915 in the Niigata Prefecture. Kamekura graduated from New Architecture and Design College in Tokyo in 1935. He was heavily influenced by the European Bauhaus movement and constructivism. He pioneered the fusion of modern Western graphic design principles with traditional Japanese aesthetic clarity during Japan's postwar recovery era.
